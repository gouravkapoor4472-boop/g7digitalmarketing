FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions About Google Ads

Find answers to common questions about paid search advertising and campaign management.

What are Google Ads?

Google Ads is Google's online advertising platform. Businesses can use different campaign types to promote their products or services across eligible Google advertising placements.

How much budget is required for Google Ads?

Your budget depends on your industry, competition, location, campaign objectives and expected cost per click or conversion. Advertising spend is separate from any campaign management fee.

How soon can Google Ads generate leads?

Ads can begin receiving traffic after approval and launch, but lead generation depends on your targeting, offer, landing page, budget and competition. Results are not guaranteed.

What is PPC in digital marketing?

PPC stands for pay-per-click, an advertising model in which an advertiser generally pays when someone clicks an ad. Google Ads also supports other bidding strategies depending on campaign type and objective.

What is conversion tracking?

Conversion tracking measures valuable actions, such as purchases, form submissions or calls, when the relevant tracking setup and platform support are available.

Are Google Ads and SEO the same?

No. Google Ads is paid advertising, while SEO focuses on improving organic search visibility. Both can support a broader digital marketing strategy.
