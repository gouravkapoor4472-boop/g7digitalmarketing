Why Choose Google Ads for Your Business?

Google Ads can help businesses appear in relevant paid search placements when potential customers look for products or services. Campaigns can be planned around suitable keywords, locations and business objectives.

Effective advertising requires more than getting clicks. Keyword relevance, ad quality, landing-page experience, competition and conversion tracking can all affect performance.

Campaign results vary by industry, location, budget and offer. No specific ranking, lead volume or sales result can be guaranteed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Google Ads campaigns.

What are Google Ads?

Google Ads is Google's advertising platform that allows businesses to promote their offers through eligible search, display and other advertising placements.

How much budget do I need for Google Ads?

The appropriate budget depends on your location, competition, keywords and business goals. Start with an affordable budget that can be measured and reviewed.

Do Google Ads guarantee leads or sales?

No. Results depend on search demand, competition, the offer, landing-page experience and campaign execution.

Is the ad budget separate from management fees?

Typically, the advertising spend paid to Google and the campaign management fee are separate costs. Confirm the agreed budget and scope before starting.
