FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions About Meta Ads

Find answers to common questions about Facebook and Instagram advertising.

What are Meta Ads?

Meta Ads are paid advertisements that businesses can run across eligible placements on platforms such as Facebook and Instagram. Campaigns can support goals such as awareness, leads or sales.

Are Facebook Ads and Instagram Ads the same?

Both can be managed through Meta's advertising tools, but the creative format, audience response and placement may differ. Campaigns should be planned around your audience and objectives.

How much budget is required for Meta Ads?

The budget depends on your goals, audience, industry and testing needs. Ad spend is separate from any management fee. A suitable starting budget should be discussed before launching a campaign.

How long does it take to see results?

Results vary based on campaign objective, budget, offer, creative, competition and tracking quality. There is no guaranteed timeline or guaranteed number of leads or sales.

Can Meta Ads generate leads for my business?

Yes, lead generation campaigns can encourage people to submit forms, send messages or contact your business. Outcomes depend on campaign setup and the quality of your offer and follow-up.

Do I need a Facebook or Instagram business account?

You generally need the appropriate Meta business assets and advertising account access to run campaigns. The exact setup depends on your business and campaign requirements.
