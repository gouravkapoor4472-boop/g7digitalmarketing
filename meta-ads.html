Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Facebook and Instagram advertising.

What are Meta Ads?

Meta Ads are paid advertising campaigns that can appear across eligible Meta placements, including Facebook and Instagram.

How much budget do I need for Meta Ads?

Your budget depends on your objective, target audience, competition and testing requirements. Choose a realistic budget that your business can afford to evaluate.

Can Meta Ads generate leads?

They can support lead generation, but actual results depend on your offer, creative, audience, follow-up and campaign setup.

Is advertising spend included in the service fee?

Advertising spend paid to Meta and campaign management fees are typically separate costs. Confirm the agreed scope and budget before starting a campaign.
