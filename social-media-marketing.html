FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about social media marketing and how it can support your business.

What is social media marketing?

Social media marketing uses platforms such as Instagram and Facebook to share content, communicate with audiences and support business goals.

Which social media platforms should my business use?

The right platforms depend on your target audience, industry, content format and objectives. It is usually better to focus on platforms that fit your business rather than trying to maintain every platform at once.

How often should I post on social media?

Posting frequency depends on your audience, resources and platform. A realistic, consistent schedule with useful content is more valuable than posting frequently without a plan.

Does social media marketing guarantee followers or sales?

No. Results depend on your content, audience, industry, competition, consistency and other factors. No specific number of followers or sales can be guaranteed.

What is the difference between social media marketing and Meta Ads?

Social media marketing can include organic content, account management and community engagement. Meta Ads refers to paid advertising across eligible Meta platforms such as Facebook and Instagram.
