Frequently Asked Questions

Web Design & Development FAQs

Learn more about website planning, development and the features your business may need.

Why does my business need a website?

A website gives customers a place to learn about your business, explore services and find contact details. It can also support your digital marketing activities.

Will my website work on mobile devices?

Responsive design helps a website adapt to mobile, tablet and desktop screens. The final result should be tested on different screen sizes.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. A redesign can improve the visual layout, content organization, navigation and mobile usability of an existing website.

Will my website be SEO-friendly?

A website can be built with SEO fundamentals such as clear headings, descriptive titles, crawlable links and a logical page structure. Rankings are not guaranteed.

How long does website development take?

The timeline depends on the number of pages, required features, content readiness and project scope. A clear plan helps estimate the time needed.

Can I update website content later?

Yes. Content can be updated through the editing method supported by your website platform. For a coded website, changes may require editing HTML or CSS.
